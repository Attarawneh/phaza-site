Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data phaza.io collects, why, who handles it and how long it is kept. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Last updated 30 September 2026

Who we are

Phaza is an artificial intelligence company with its headquarters in Amman, Jordan, at the Housing Bank Complex, Queen Noor Street, Shmeisani, and a branch in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Phaza is the controller of the personal data described here.

For anything in this policy, write to support@phaza.io.

Our position, before the detail

Phaza exists because institutions should not have to surrender their data to use intelligence. Every model we build is delivered to run inside the customer's own walls: the weights transfer with the deal, inference stays behind their perimeter, and nothing calls home. A company that argues this for a living cannot run its own website on a different principle.

So this site is built to the same rule, and you can verify most of it yourself:

  • No cookies. None — which is why there is no consent banner to dismiss.
  • No third-party trackers, no advertising pixels, no social media embeds, no session recording, no heatmaps, no data brokers.
  • No third-party fonts. Our typefaces are served from our own domain, so no font network receives your address as a side effect of reading this page.
  • Nothing stored in your browser. No local storage, no fingerprinting.
  • We ask for the minimum. The forms request what is needed to answer you and nothing more; there is no field on this site whose purpose is to build a profile of you.

Open the developer tools and watch the network while you read. The absence of requests is the argument.

What this covers

The phaza.io website and the forms on it.

It does not cover the systems we build for customers. Those run inside the customer's own environment, on their infrastructure, under their law and their rules: the customer is the controller of everything that passes through them, and Phaza holds no copy of their operational data, their prompts or their model's answers. Where a deployment is air-gapped, we could not reach that data if we were asked to. Any exception is written into the contract for that engagement, never assumed here.

It also does not cover other companies' websites we link to.

What we collect

When you contact us

The inquiry form asks for, and sends us:

  • Your name and email address, so we can reply.
  • Your organisation, role and country, so the inquiry reaches the right people here.
  • The subject and your message — whatever you choose to write.

Please do not put confidential or sensitive material in that message. It is an ordinary business inbox, not a secure channel; if something needs protecting, tell us and we will arrange a proper one.

When you apply for a job

  • Your CV, as a PDF or Word file, and whatever it contains.
  • Your contact details and any answers you give about the role.

Applications are read and assessed by our recruitment process, which includes automated screening of the CV against the requirements of the role. No hiring decision is made by a machine alone — a person reviews every outcome, and you can ask for a human explanation of any assessment.

When you simply visit

  • Technical request data — your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time — recorded by the services that deliver and protect this site, as happens with any website.
  • Aggregate page statistics, collected by Cloudflare's privacy-focused analytics. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint you and does not follow you across other sites.

This site sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and carries no advertising or social-media trackers. That is why you have never seen a cookie banner here.

Why we are allowed to hold it

  • Because you asked us to. You filled in a form to reach us, and we use what you sent to answer.
  • To take steps towards a contract — responding to an inquiry, or considering a job application you submitted.
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping the site working, secure and free of abuse, and in keeping a record of business correspondence.
  • Legal obligation, where a law requires us to keep something.

Who else handles it

We keep the list short on purpose. Personal data from this site is handled by us and by the service providers we depend on to run it:

  • Our hosting and content delivery providers, who serve the site and absorb attacks against it.
  • Our email and business systems, where inquiries and applications arrive and are worked on.
  • Our own infrastructure, which receives the forms.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or marketing networks. We do not use it to train models — not ours, not anyone's.

How we handle what you send

An inquiry from this site reaches our own systems, and is worked on by the people whose job it is to answer it. Access is granted by role and reviewed; nobody has standing access to everything because it is convenient. Personal data is not copied into analytics tools, enrichment services or marketing platforms, and it is not combined with data bought from anyone.

It is also not used to train any model — not Salam, not an internal tool, not a vendor's system. We build our training corpus from content Phaza creates under clear agreements with the people who contribute to it, and we do not exempt our own inbox from that principle.

What we will never do

  • Sell, rent or trade personal data.
  • Share it with advertisers, data brokers or marketing networks.
  • Use your inquiry or your CV as training data.
  • Track you across other websites.
  • Add trackers to this site in exchange for a metric.

These are not aspirations pending a policy review. If any of them ever changed, the change would be stated on this page in plain words and dated.

Where it goes

We operate from Jordan and the United Arab Emirates, and our providers operate internationally, so data may be processed outside your country. Where that happens we rely on the safeguards the relevant law requires, and we hold providers to written terms covering confidentiality and security.

How long we keep it

  • Inquiries: up to 24 months after our last exchange, then deleted — unless the conversation became a business relationship, in which case it is kept as part of that record.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months after a decision, so we can come back to you if something suitable opens. Ask us and we will delete it sooner.
  • Technical logs: short retention by our providers, for security and diagnostics.

Your rights

Depending on where you are, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete it;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • withdraw consent, where we relied on consent;
  • have a person review any automated assessment of your application.

Write to support@phaza.io. We will answer within 30 days. If we cannot do what you ask, we will tell you why. You may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Our handling is intended to meet Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law, the personal data protection law of the United Arab Emirates, and the GDPR where it applies to visitors from the European Union.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. A strict content security policy limits what the page is permitted to load or contact at all, so a compromised third party has nowhere to run. The application code is delivered with cryptographic integrity checks: if a single byte were altered anywhere between our origin and your browser, it would refuse to execute rather than run quietly. Forms are protected against automated abuse without profiling the people who use them.

Internally, access to inquiries and applications is limited by role, credentials are rotated, and administrative access is accounted for. We make no claim to a certification we do not hold; what we will do is answer a security questionnaire honestly and let a customer's own team examine the architecture.

No system is perfect. If we discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you and the relevant authority within the time the law allows, and we will say what happened rather than what sounds best.

Children

This site is meant for professional and institutional audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us something, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

When this policy changes we update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle personal data already given to us, we will say so plainly rather than quietly editing the page.
